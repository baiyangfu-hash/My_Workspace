📍 街头问路 (Asking Directions)

场景分类: 交通出行 • 适用场景: 寻找最近的地铁站
Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to the nearest subway station?
打扰一下，请问最近的地铁站怎么走？
Sure! Go straight for two blocks, then turn left at the traffic light. It's right next to the bank.
好的！直走过两个街区，然后在红绿灯处左转。它就在银行旁边。
Is it within walking distance?
步行能到吗？
Yes, it only takes about five minutes on foot.
是的，步行大约只需要五分钟。
